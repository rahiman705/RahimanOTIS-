# Vendor Coordination

## Trigger phrases
- I need to coordinate with the vendor
- Vendor support is required
- What does the vendor need to do
- Prepare tasks for the vendor
- The vendor is visiting the site
- Prepare for the vendor visit
- Check vendor prerequisites
- We are waiting for the vendor
- What information should I send the vendor

## Flow
1. Identify vendor and requested work.
2. Identify prerequisites and dependencies.
3. Separate vendor actions, internal IT actions, site actions and requester actions.
4. Capture visit date, access needs and site contact when available.
5. Do not invent vendor commitments, contacts, dates or service levels.
6. Present what is waiting on whom and the next action.
